How the scoring works

The quiz measures hidden gameplay preferences rather than surface aesthetics. Each answer contributes to dimensions such as burst payoff, sustained pressure, reactive play, deliberate planning, precision, setup, maintenance, utility, control, support, resource management, target commitment, adaptability, attrition, durability, and management of a separate combat contributor.

Every WoW Forever tree has a hand-authored profile on those dimensions based on its currently revealed Forever talents and intended gameplay hooks. Your answers are converted into a preference vector and compared with all 27 tree profiles using cosine similarity. If you select multiple answers on one question, that question's weight is divided evenly among the selections, so checking three boxes does not score three times as much as checking one.

The result is a playstyle match, not a DPS ranking, tier list, raid-value ranking, or prediction of final beta tuning.

Source basis: Blizzard's WoW Forever announcement and the current BlizzCon-demo talent transcriptions at wowclassicforever.info. The demo data covers 470 talents across 9 classes, with 143 talents new to those Classic-frame trees and 15 of 27 trees receiving a different 31-point talent.

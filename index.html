How the scoring works

1. Each question is internally centered. An answer is interpreted relative to the other choices in that question, so an option cannot score highly merely because its raw vector contains more numbers.

2. Multi-select expresses uncertainty. One selection carries full evidence, two carry less, three carry little, and all four are neutral. Broad answers therefore reduce certainty rather than inflate scores.

3. The 27 tree profiles are standardized dimension by dimension. A trait only separates a tree when that tree is unusually high or low in it relative to the Forever roster. Profiles are hand-coded from the currently revealed mechanics and are not trained on player outcome data.

4. Matching uses cosine similarity. This compares the direction of your preference profile with each standardized tree profile without rewarding a tree simply for having more high-valued traits.

5. Round 2 is adaptive. Candidate questions are simulated against your current top five; the six expected to create the most useful separation are chosen, with a penalty for asking near-duplicate questions.

6. Result stability is tested. The final page removes each informative question one at a time and recalculates the winner. This shows how dependent the result is on any single answer.

7. No score is a probability. Results are reported as standard deviations above or below your average match across the 27 trees, plus answer specificity and leave-one-question-out stability. Close results are explicitly reported as ties.

The result is a playstyle recommendation, not a DPS ranking, raid-value ranking, psychological test, or guarantee of final beta tuning.
